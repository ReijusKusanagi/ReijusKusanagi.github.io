WHO THEY ARE

From humble beginnings to trusted catering service since 2022

A story rooted in Passion, Growth and Dedication

Riz's Event Catering Services began its journey in 2022 as a humble provider of short-order meals. As the business steadily grew, so did the trust and demand from clients — eventually leading to requests for full catering services. What started small soon evolved into a dedicated catering service, marking the beginning of a new chapter for the brand.

The name "Riz" holds a special meaning, inspired by the unica hija of Riza and Louie. It reflects the heart, passion, and personal touch behind every service they provide.

Today, Riz's Event Catering Services continues to offer quality food and reliable event solutions, committed to making every celebration memorable through carefully prepared dishes and attentive service.

Clients Served

We are proud to be part of our clients' special moments — from intimate family gatherings to large celebrations. Riz's Event Catering Services has had the pleasure of serving a variety of events, delivering quality food and reliable service for every occasion.

Locations Served

From private homes to event venues across Pampanga, Riz's Event Catering Services has catered to a wide range of locations — bringing the same level of quality, service, and attention to detail wherever the celebration takes place.
